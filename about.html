Our Story

BeerHole™ didn't start in a boardroom. It started in a garage, the same place most great ideas do.

How It Started

Built From The Ground Up

BeerHole™ was invented by us — a group of friends who love playing games and feed off of competition. After a summer of utilizing yard games to settle beef and claim bragging rights, we invented the ultimate game of skill. By applying the classic Beer Pong rules to the mechanics and setup of Cornhole, BeerHole™ was born.

What started as an idea in our garage turned into a game that immediately hooked in everyone who played. The rules came naturally. The competition was instant. The fun was undeniable. We knew we had something.

We built the first sets by hand, tested them with friends, refined elements of the product, and kept pushing until BeerHole™ was exactly what we knew it could be.

Who Makes It

Piggy Pit Woodworking

Every BeerHole™ set is manufactured by Piggy Pit Woodworking — a craftsman operation that is committed to professional level carpentry and quality. Each board is built with exquisite joinery techniques and an impeccable finish. This ensures every set is made to last and made to perform.

When we set out to bring BeerHole™ to life, we didn't want something mass-produced and forgettable. Partnering with Piggy Pit means every set that leaves the shop is something we're proud to put our name on.
